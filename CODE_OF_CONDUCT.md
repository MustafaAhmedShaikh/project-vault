# Code of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1. The full text is here:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

## Short version

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are new to open source.
- Welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, or unwelcome personal attention.
- Do not publish other people's private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or ban people who break these rules.

To report a problem privately, open the repository's **Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability**, and put "Code of Conduct" at the start of the title. Only the maintainers can see these reports. Please do not post the details in a public issue. If that option is not available, open a public issue that only says you need a private way to report a conduct problem, with no details, and a maintainer will arrange one.
